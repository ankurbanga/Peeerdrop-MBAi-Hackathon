# Peerdrop design
A quiet personal address book organized as a constellation. The network is the main content, with a compact search rail and an accessible list companion. Mobile uses full-width canvas and a bottom navigation; desktop gains a left rail and right results column. Purple marks action and self; warm neutral surfaces keep people prominent. Use locally bundled Geist sans, 4/8px spacing, 14px panels, 44px controls, low contrast graph lines. Sheets protect focused exchange and note entry. Privacy labels are literal. No decorative metrics, gradients, or fake event links.
