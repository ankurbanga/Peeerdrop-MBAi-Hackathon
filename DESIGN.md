# Peerdrop design

A quiet personal address book organized as a constellation. The network is the main content, with a compact search rail and an accessible list companion. Mobile uses full-width canvas and a bottom navigation; desktop gains a left rail and right results column. A smiling purple droplet carries the brand, and realistic fictional headshots make the demo network feel human; initials remain the fallback. Purple marks action and self; warm neutral surfaces keep people prominent. Use locally bundled Geist sans, 4/8px spacing, 14px panels, 44px controls, low contrast graph lines. The slim network snapshot reports cohort reach, first-degree connections, second-years, and the private close network without turning the page into a dashboard. Sheets protect focused exchange and note entry. Privacy labels are literal. No decorative metrics, gradients, or fake event links.
