# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Supabase, React Flow. Specified in docs/TECHNICAL_SPEC.md.

## Users

New Kellogg students remembering people met during orientation.

## Product Purpose

Remember the person. Restart the conversation. Reciprocal selective exchange preserves context for later fragment-based recall.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Real shared persistence; explicit reciprocal consent; required cohort year; private close-network favorites; no campus integrations; fictional demo fixtures and headshots; private notes remain private. Anonymous browser identity has no recovery.

## Brand Commitments

Peerdrop; restrained purple, light background, clear typography, avatar graph, minimal animation.

## Evidence on Hand

PRD and technical spec. No established pilot results or commercial claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

390px mobile layout, keyboard nodes, accessible result list, 44px touch controls, visible focus.
